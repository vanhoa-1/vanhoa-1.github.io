🛡️ Staying Safe With AI

Good practice matters more than any single tool. The guide returns to these points repeatedly.

  • Never paste passwords, account numbers, medical records, or private customer details into a public AI chat window.
  • Assume anything you type may be stored and reviewed. If you would not email it to a stranger, do not type it.
  • Ask for sources whenever a claim matters, and open those sources yourself.
  • Keep a human in the loop for anything that affects money, health, legal matters, or another person's rights.
  • Tell people when content was produced with AI assistance, especially in professional or academic settings.

💡 Tips for Getting Better Results

  • Be specific. "Summarize this in five bullet points for a busy manager" beats "summarize this."
  • Give an example. Showing the style you want works far better than describing it.
  • Iterate. The first answer is a starting point. Ask for a shorter version, a simpler version, or a version aimed at a different reader.
  • Ask it to think out loud. Requesting a short explanation of the reasoning makes mistakes easier to spot.
  • Keep your own notes. When a request works well, save it. A small personal library of proven prompts pays off for years.
  • Know when to stop. If you have gone three rounds without progress, the tool may simply not have the information you need.
